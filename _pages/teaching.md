During my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, I served as the **Instructor of Record** for two undergraduate courses — taking full responsibility for lectures, course design, assignments and exams, automated grading setup, and supervising the teaching assistants.

### Instructor of Record — Virginia Tech, Blacksburg, VA

**CS 4104 Data and Algorithm Analysis** — Instructor of Record (Summer 2025)  
*Design and analysis of efficient algorithms: greedy algorithms, divide and conquer, dynamic programming, and network flow, with rigorous correctness and running-time analysis. Taught online.*  
Syllabus: [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Xk4B3G4vPZO7j5pKMZNbc2wanW-dyVK4/view?usp=sharing)

**CS 1044 Introduction to Programming in C++** — Instructor of Record (Spring 2025)  
*First programming course covering variables, control flow, functions, arrays, and file I/O in C++, with weekly hands-on coding and automated grading. Taught in person.*  
Syllabus: [Link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qVxbSU0jTX1VQ6ewCBRUkC0QlXwws2_b/view?usp=sharing)
